## 1. About this policy

1.1 This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (ABN [ABN]) ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**") collects, uses, discloses, stores and otherwise handles personal information. We trade as [TRADING NAME].

1.2 This policy applies when you:

(a) visit or interact with our marketing website at [WEBSITE URL] (the "**Website**");

(b) use our inspection management software, [PRODUCT NAME], at [APP URL] (the "**Platform**");

(c) contact us for sales, support or other enquiries; or

(d) otherwise deal with us, including as a customer, prospective customer, supplier or job applicant.

1.3 We handle personal information in line with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) (the "**Privacy Act**") and the Australian Privacy Principles ("**APPs**"). If our turnover means we would otherwise be exempt from the Privacy Act as a small business, we have chosen to comply with the APPs anyway.

1.4 If we provide services to people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or California, we also meet our obligations under the laws that apply there. Section 16 and section 17 explain the additional rights available in those places.

1.5 In this policy, "**personal information**" means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable. This applies whether the information is true or not, and whether or not it is recorded in a material form. Where the GDPR applies, this includes "personal data".

## 2. Our role: when we decide and when our customers decide

2.1 **Where we decide how information is used.** We are responsible for the personal information we collect for our own business purposes. Examples include:

(a) Website visitors and enquiries;

(b) sales, marketing and CRM contacts;

(c) billing contacts and account administrators;

(d) Platform user account details (name, email address, login and security records); and

(e) support requests and communications with us.

2.2 **Where we act for our customers.** Our customers are organisations, such as contractors, asset owners and inspection service providers. They upload and manage inspection data in the Platform. This "**Customer Data**" can include inspection records, test results, photographs, documents, inspector names, qualifications, signatures and comments.

2.3 We handle Customer Data on behalf of the relevant customer and under our written agreement with that customer. In those cases:

(a) the customer controls the Customer Data;

(b) the customer is responsible for having a lawful basis to collect it and for giving notice to the individuals concerned; and

(c) we use the Customer Data only to provide, secure, support and improve the Platform for that customer, or as required by law.

2.4 If you are an individual whose information appears in Customer Data (for example, an inspector or site worker), please send privacy requests to the organisation that uses the Platform, such as your employer or the contractor you work with. If you contact us directly, we will refer your request to that organisation and help it respond where appropriate.

## 3. Personal information we collect

3.1 **Information you give us.** Depending on how you deal with us, this may include:

(a) **Contact details:** name, job title, employer, business email, phone number and business address;

(b) **Account details:** username, email, password (stored only in hashed form), role and permissions, multi-factor authentication settings, and the organisation and site or project you are associated with;

(c) **Professional information:** qualifications, certifications, competency or licence numbers, and inspector identifiers, where these are entered into the Platform;

(d) **Content you create:** inspection records, comments, photographs, uploaded files, electronic signatures and approvals;

(e) **Commercial information:** billing contacts, purchase orders, invoices, payment history, and contract and e-signature records. We do not store full payment card numbers ourselves. Card payments are handled by our payment provider;

(f) **Communications:** support tickets, emails, call notes, chat messages, feedback and survey responses; and

(g) **Recruitment information:** if you apply to work with us, your CV, work history, referees and related information.

3.2 **Information we collect automatically.** When you use the Website or Platform, we may collect:

(a) **Device and log data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, pages viewed, date and time of access, and error logs;

(b) **Platform usage and audit data:** login times, actions performed, records created, edited or approved, and related timestamps. Audit trails are an important part of inspection record integrity;

(c) **Approximate location:** location inferred from IP address. We do not collect precise device location unless a feature clearly asks for it and you agree. If a feature adds location to a photo or record, we will tell you; and

(d) **Cookies and similar technologies:** see section 9.

3.3 **Information from third parties.** We may collect personal information from:

(a) your employer or organisation, for example when an administrator creates a user account for you;

(b) our customers, as part of Customer Data;

(c) single sign-on or identity providers, if your organisation uses them;

(d) our service providers, such as payment, analytics, CRM and e-signature providers;

(e) public sources, such as company registers, business websites and professional networking sites, for business-to-business sales purposes; and

(f) event organisers, partners or referrers, where you have agreed to share your details.

3.4 **Sensitive information.** We do not intend to collect sensitive information, such as health information or information about racial or ethnic origin, criminal record or biometric templates. We ask customers not to upload sensitive information to the Platform unless it is reasonably necessary and lawful. If we need to collect sensitive information for our own purposes, we will ask for your consent unless an exception under the Privacy Act applies.

3.5 **Government identifiers.** We do not use government-related identifiers, such as tax file numbers or driver licence numbers, as our own identifiers for you.

3.6 **Unsolicited information.** If we receive personal information we did not ask for and could not have collected under this policy, we will destroy or de-identify it as soon as practicable, where it is lawful and reasonable to do so.

## 4. Anonymity and pseudonymity

4.1 You can browse the Website and make general enquiries without telling us who you are, or by using a pseudonym.

4.2 You cannot use the Platform anonymously. Inspection records must be attributable to identified users for audit, quality assurance and regulatory reasons. We also need your contact details to provide an account, support, billing or contracts.

## 5. How we collect personal information

5.1 We collect personal information in lawful and fair ways. We collect it directly from you where reasonably practicable. This includes collection through web forms, account registration, the Platform, email, phone, video calls, events and contract processes.

5.2 Sometimes we collect information from third parties, as described in section 3.3. Where we do, we take reasonable steps to make sure you know about it, unless it is obvious from the circumstances. For example, an administrator at your employer may set up your account.

## 6. Why we use personal information

6.1 We collect, hold, use and disclose personal information for the following purposes:

(a) **Providing the Platform:** creating and managing accounts, authenticating users, hosting and processing Customer Data, generating reports and keeping audit trails;

(b) **Customer support:** responding to enquiries, troubleshooting, maintaining a help centre and knowledge base, and onboarding and training;

(c) **Contracts and billing:** preparing proposals and contracts, collecting e-signatures, invoicing, processing payments and managing accounts;

(d) **Security and integrity:** detecting, investigating and preventing fraud, unauthorised access, misuse and security incidents, and keeping backups and disaster recovery arrangements;

(e) **Improving our services:** analysing how the Website and Platform are used, fixing bugs, and developing new features. Where practicable, we use aggregated or de-identified information for this;

(f) **Communications:** sending service announcements, security notices, release notes and other administrative messages;

(g) **Marketing:** sending information about our products, events and updates where permitted (see section 8);

(h) **Legal and compliance:** meeting legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations, responding to lawful requests, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims;

(i) **Business operations:** managing our relationships with customers, suppliers and partners, and dealing with recruitment; and

(j) **Corporate transactions:** if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality obligations.

6.2 We use and disclose personal information only for the primary purpose for which we collected it, or for a related secondary purpose you would reasonably expect. Otherwise, we will ask for your consent or rely on an exception permitted by law.

6.3 **Customer Data.** We do not use Customer Data for marketing. We do not sell Customer Data. We do not use identifiable Customer Data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

6.4 **Automated decisions.** We do not currently make decisions that significantly affect individuals' rights or interests using only automated processing. If we introduce such features, we will update this policy to explain the kinds of personal information used and the kinds of decisions made.

## 10. Storage and security

10.1 We hold personal information electronically. Production Platform data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in [AWS REGION, e.g. Sydney, Australia]. Some of our service providers may store or access information in other locations (see section 11).

10.2 We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure. These steps include:

(a) encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest;

(b) role-based access controls, least-privilege access and multi-factor authentication for administrative access;

(c) logical separation of each customer's data;

(d) security logging, monitoring and audit trails;

(e) regular backups and tested recovery procedures;

(f) vulnerability management, secure development practices and timely patching;

(g) keeping production and development environments separate. We do not use identifiable production Customer Data in development or test environments unless it is strictly necessary, authorised by the customer and appropriately protected;

(h) confidentiality obligations and privacy and security training for staff and contractors; and

(i) due diligence and contractual protections for service providers.

## 14. Data breaches

14.1 We have a data breach response plan. If we suspect a data breach, we act quickly to contain it, assess it and reduce any harm.

14.2 Under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme in Part IIIC of the Privacy Act, some breaches must be reported. If a breach is likely to result in serious harm to the individuals affected, we will notify those individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner ("**OAIC**").

14.3 If a breach involves Customer Data, we will notify the affected customer without undue delay, as set out in our customer agreement, and work with them on any notifications required. Where other laws apply (for example, the GDPR), we will meet the notification requirements of those laws.
