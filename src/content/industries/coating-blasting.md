## Surface treatment quality

Project AIP is designed to help teams capture coating and blasting checks against the relevant asset, work pack and inspection plan.

- **Environmental checks:** record ambient temperature, relative humidity, dew point and steel temperature.
- **Surface preparation:** capture cleanliness, profile and contamination checks.
- **Coating application:** associate WFT/DFT readings, product batch information and supporting photographs with the inspected item.

Acceptance criteria must come from the approved project specification and applicable standards.
