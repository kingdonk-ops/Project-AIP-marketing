## Designed around your coating specification

Whether you are running surface preparation inspections in a Pilbara shut or verifying high-build epoxy on an offshore LNG module, Project AIP follows the sequence in your approved procedure: ambient checks, blast, profile and cleanliness, hold point, stripe coat, film build, final DFT and holiday test.

- **Hold after blasting:** no coat is applied until the profile and cleanliness line is released.
- **Readings with provenance:** every WFT and DFT run records the gauge serial and calibration status.
- **NDT in the same record:** MPI, DPI, UT and visual results sit against the same asset as the coating history.

Acceptance criteria must come from the approved project specification and applicable standards. Project AIP records and enforces them; it doesn't set them.
