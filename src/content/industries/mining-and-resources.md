## Shuts run on hold points

On a shut, every hour a crew waits for an inspection sign-off costs money, and every sign-off skipped costs more later. Project AIP makes the hold points visible against the work packs they gate, so superintendents see what's blocked and why.

Inspection stages and acceptance criteria should be configured from the approved ITP and project standards.
