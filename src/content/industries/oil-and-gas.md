## QA/QC for pressure systems

Pressure piping QA is a chain of evidence: material certificates, qualified procedures, qualified welders, NDT, repairs, PWHT, and finally the test. Project AIP keeps that chain attached to the asset, so the test pack and the MDR are assembled from records that already exist.

Code requirements and acceptance criteria are set by your project specification and the applicable codes. Project AIP records and enforces them; it doesn't set them.
