Quality roll-up gives you a live view of quality across a project without double entry. It reads directly from inspections, ITPs, NCRs and punch items, so ITP completion, open NCRs, punch ageing, first-time pass rates and cost of poor quality are always current — and every KPI drills down to the records behind it.

Alongside the dashboard sits a small **audit register** for internal, external and supplier audits. Findings link to issues and corrective actions, closure is verified, and audits can be mapped to ISO 9001 clauses to support your quality management programme.

## Key features

- Quality dashboard filtered by project, site, discipline, subcontractor and period, with trends and clear data definitions
- Audit register with findings linked to issues, closure verification and clause mapping
- Cost of poor quality from rework hours and cost, grouped by category, discipline and subcontractor
- First-time pass rate by discipline, inspector and subcontractor, with a consistent, visible definition
- Clause-mapped evidence pack built from approved ITPs, NCR closures, calibration and competency records
- Repeat-defect and root-cause trends by asset class, coating system or CUI mechanism
- Audit sampling of random closed inspections and hold points
- Quality objectives and targets with RAG status per project
- Client-facing quality snapshot that shares approved KPIs without exposing internal NCR detail
- Scheduled quality reports and notifications for audits and findings
