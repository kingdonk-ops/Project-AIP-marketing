Punch items capture the snags found at completion — each with a photo, its location on the asset tree and optionally a pin on the drawing, a category, a priority, a due date and the company responsible for fixing it.

Every item follows a **verify-before-close** workflow: the person who fixed it can't also sign it off, and verification needs evidence. After handover, the very same record continues as a defect through the defects liability period, so history follows the asset and retention release can be tied to verified closure.

## Key features

- Punch items with photo, drawing pin, asset, responsible party, due date, category and priority
- Independent verification with evidence; reject and reopen when a fix isn't right
- Defects liability periods per project or contract, with configurable start and end dates
- Post-handover defects assigned to the contractor or subcontractor responsible
- Retention release gated on verified closure
- Mobile walkdown mode with area progress, offline capture, voice notes and assign-later
- Bulk assign, reschedule, verify and export
- Create items from minor inspection failures, and escalate serious ones to an NCR
- Export per area or contractor (PDF and spreadsheet) for the handover pack
- Reminders and overdue notifications
- Your own terminology — Punch, Snag, Defect or Deficiency
