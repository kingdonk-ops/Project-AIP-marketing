The Temporary Works Register governs scaffolds, propping, excavation support and other temporary works through a recognised staged approach: design brief, design and check, permit to load, inspections while in use, and permit to strike.

Each item is registered with its category, risk class and the asset it accesses or supports. The designer and checker must be different people, periodic inspections are scheduled automatically, and dependent work is held until permits and valid tags are in place. Stage names follow your own terminology.

## Key features

- Register with category, risk class, asset link, coordinator and stage chips, plus a board view by stage
- Design brief, design and calculations, and independent check records
- Designer and checker separation enforced by the system
- Permit to load and permit to strike with sign-off and conditions checklist
- Periodic inspection schedule per category, such as weekly scaffold tags, with overdue alerts
- Hold flags that block related ITP steps or scope tasks until a permit is issued
- Scaffold tag status linked to scope tasks as a work gate
- Scaffold handover certificate and weekly inspection as standard inspection types
- Loading class and capacity, with warnings for insulation or equipment loads
- Modification and dismantle requests that re-trigger inspection and reset permits
