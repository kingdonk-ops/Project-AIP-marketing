Service & Maintenance picks up where handover ends. Hold service contracts with SLA targets, raise tickets and work orders against assets in the hierarchy, schedule preventive maintenance and recurring inspections, and track performance against the contract.

It is built for inspection, repair and re-coating campaigns rather than as a general-purpose maintenance system. Findings from inspections flow straight into repair work orders, and every asset keeps one lifecycle history from construction ITPs through to the latest service visit.

## Key features

- Service contracts with client, term, scope and SLA targets
- Tickets and work orders with tasks, labour, materials and sign-off
- Preventive maintenance schedules by asset or asset class, with frequency and next due date
- Risk-based inspection intervals recalculated from the last finding
- Work orders created automatically from findings above a severity threshold, with photos and recommended repair
- Recurring campaign templates with stage gates and ITP binding
- Technician assignment checked against skills and certificates
- SLA clocks that pause for client hold or access restrictions, with the reason recorded
- Defects from the liability period converted into work orders
- Asset history timeline merging construction, handover, inspections and work orders
