Comments, Mentions & Notifications gives every part of the platform threaded discussion on any record or file — with @mentions, resolve and reopen, and pins on PDF pages and photos. Because comments stay attached to the asset as well as the record, an asset carries its **full discussion history** across inspections, NCRs and RFIs.

One notification service tells people what they need to know, in-app, by email or by mobile push, with per-user preferences and daily digests. Rules by asset area, discipline and severity route alerts to the right person, quiet hours respect site shifts, and critical items such as a waiting hold point or expired certificate still escalate.

## Key features

- Threaded comments with @mentions, resolve and reopen
- Pins and viewpoints on PDF pages, drawings and photos
- Comments on files scoped to a file version and carried forward on request
- Visibility classes — internal, shared with client, shared with subcontractor — with clear badges
- Asset-level discussion history rolled up from inspections, NCRs and RFIs
- Convert a comment into a task, issue or corrective action, with a back-link
- Edit history and superseding comments; edits never overwrite
- In-app notification centre, email and mobile push, with digests, snooze and mute
- Notification rules by asset area, discipline and severity
- Quiet hours and shift awareness, with escalation for critical items and mandatory notices
