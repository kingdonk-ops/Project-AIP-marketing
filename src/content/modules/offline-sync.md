## Designed for remote work

Industrial maintenance and construction teams often work in areas with intermittent connectivity. Project AIP is designed around field capture that can continue offline, then reconcile with the central record when a connection returns.

### Dual-track offline model

- **Cached reference material:** approved drawings, ITPs and specifications can remain available according to configured retention policies.
- **Provisional field records:** offline entries are identified as provisional until synchronization and applicable review checks are complete.

Offline approval windows, retention and reconciliation rules are configurable policy decisions and should be validated against project requirements before deployment.
