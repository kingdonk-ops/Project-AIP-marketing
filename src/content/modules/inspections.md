Inspections are the flagship of Project AIP. Every inspection is filled in against a specific asset using a frozen template revision, so the record always shows exactly what was asked and answered.

Inspection test plans (ITPs) and RFIs are inspection types whose **hold points gate downstream work** until they are formally released. Records move through a full review chain with permissions at every step, can require client sign-off, and can be scheduled with inspectors and customers notified automatically.

## What teams get

- Hold, witness and surveillance points that behave the way contracts expect
- Failed steps that link straight to an NCR and re-inspection
- Coverage and backlog views showing what is done, pending and overdue
