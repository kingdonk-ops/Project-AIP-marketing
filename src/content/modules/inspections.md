Inspections are the central record of the platform. Each one is a template — pinned to the exact revision it started on — filled in against an asset, so every answer, photo and signature sits in that asset's history. Inspection and test plans are built from the same record, with steps linked to real scope tasks and assets.

Every ITP step carries a point type that changes how it behaves. **Hold points** block the work that follows until they are released with a signed release-to-proceed record. **Witness points** notify the client with a notice period and record a waiver if nobody attends. Review and surveillance points sit alongside, with surveillance sampled rather than mandatory on every item.

Inspections move through a full review chain — inspector, supervisor and optionally the client — with role-based permissions at every step. Before each sign-off, an eligibility check confirms the signer is qualified, the instrument is in calibration and the material is in date. Failed answers raise issues automatically, approval generates the report, and corrections are recorded as superseding entries rather than edits.

## Key features

- Full lifecycle from draft through assignment, inspector and supervisor review to optional client review, with reject, resend and re-inspect
- ITP steps with hold, witness, review and surveillance points linked to scope tasks
- Inspection notice and waiver workflow with timers, reminders and a recorded waiver
- Signed release-to-proceed records tied to the hold and the downstream tasks
- Multi-party sign-off showing who signed and how strongly they were authenticated
- Eligibility banner before each step for signer credentials, calibration and material use-by
- Customer and inspector scheduling with calendar invitations, reminders and rescheduling
- Inspection programmes with frequency, next due date and calendar, certificate-expiry, issue or ad hoc triggers
- Inspection register across assets with filters and bulk assign, approve, reject, reschedule and export
- Review queue with next and previous navigation and bulk actions
- Competency-aware assignment suggestions based on valid qualifications and availability
- Coverage and backlog dashboard by scope, area or discipline
