Roles, Permissions & Teams is the single access layer for the platform. Roles draw on one permission catalogue, users get project-scoped roles with optional overrides, and team membership limits which records people can see — for example, a subcontractor team sees only its own work. Asset-subtree scope limits a user or team to part of the asset tree, and discipline and method qualifications decide who may inspect or sign.

The same rules apply consistently to screens, search, dashboards, exports, files, comments and AI features, and access is denied by default. Commercial records such as variations, claims and supplier pricing are kept from other parties' subcontractor teams.

## Key features

- Permission catalogue per module and action, with default and custom roles
- Project-scoped roles, per-project permission matrix and per-user overrides
- Teams with visibility rules by module, record type and asset subtree
- Asset-subtree scope with inheritance and explicit exclusions
- Discipline-and-method qualified sign-off linked to current competency
- Field masking per party to hide rates, internal notes and personal data
- Effective-access explorer showing why a user can or can't act on a record
- Time-boxed delegation and acting-in-role, fully audited
- Permission matrix and quarterly access review exports; every privilege change logged
- Actions the user can't perform are hidden or disabled, not left to fail
