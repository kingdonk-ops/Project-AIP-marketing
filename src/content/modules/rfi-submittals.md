RFIs & Submittals handles formal questions to the client or designer and contractor submissions that go through review. Every RFI and submittal can be tied to an asset and its inspection or NCR history, so an inspector can see **open queries on an asset** and their effect on pending inspections before a hold point release is attempted.

RFIs carry a question, discipline, drawing references, ball-in-court, due date, response and impact flags. Submittals — shop drawings, product data, method statements, certificates, test reports and inspection paperwork — track review codes and resubmissions. A required submittal can block an inspection or task until it reaches an accepted review code.

## Key features

- RFIs with discipline, assets, package, drawing references, ball-in-court, due date and response
- Cost and schedule impact flags, and conversion to a variation candidate
- Submittal register with types, spec reference, revision, required-by date and review codes
- Configurable review outcome codes such as approved, approved as noted, revise and resubmit
- Resubmission history and side-by-side comparison
- Submittal types for weld and NDT procedures, personnel qualifications, calibration records and ITP approvals
- Gate rules that block an inspection or task until a required submittal is accepted
- Linked documents and markups, including photo markup on the RFI
- Asset-area filtering, ageing columns and overdue tracking
- Response-time analytics by party, discipline and asset area
