Safety & HSE records incidents, near misses and observations with photos, location and the asset involved. Risk is scored from your configured matrix, corrective actions are tracked to closure, and a notification clock helps keep regulator-notifiable incidents on time.

The basic module is the standard offer. For sites that need more, an advanced HSE pack can be switched on per site, adding permits to work, JSAs, toolbox talks, PPE issue, HSE audits and KPIs. Where you already run a dedicated HSE system, records can reference it to avoid double entry.

## Key features

- Incident, near-miss and observation reports with photos, location and asset
- Risk scoring from a configurable likelihood and consequence matrix
- Corrective actions on the shared issues engine
- Investigation form with root cause and contributing factors
- Regulator-notifiable flag with jurisdiction rules and deadline tracking
- Injury and health details masked at field level, with restricted exports and offline caches
- Offline mobile quick report that syncs safely when back in coverage
- Stop-work flag that places a hold on linked tasks and ITP steps
- Advanced pack per site: permit to work with competency checks, JSA/JHA, toolbox talks, PPE register and HSE audits
- Lagging and leading KPIs such as TRIR and LTIFR, using hours worked from resources
