Equipment & Fleet is a register of owned and hired plant, vehicles and test instruments — from light vehicles and compressors to UT gauges, DFT gauges and holiday detectors. Each item carries its calibration certificates, maintenance schedule and service history.

Calibration ties directly into the eligibility gate: an instrument that is out of calibration is **blocked from use**, so readings can't be taken with it. Because measurements record the instrument used, you can always show which gauge measured what.

## Key features

- Register of plant, vehicles and test instruments, owned or hired
- Calibration certificates with expiry tracking
- Out-of-calibration instruments blocked from use
- Maintenance schedules and service history
- Vehicle pre-start and plant inspections built as form templates
- Internal hire charging of equipment to projects
- Traceability of which instrument took each measurement
