The asset tree sits at the centre of the platform. Every inspection, issue, document, certificate, calibration record, NCR and photo hangs off an asset, and the tree belongs to your organisation rather than to a single project — so an asset's full history follows it from construction through shutdowns and into service, instead of being scattered across project files.

Build the tree by hand or by import, using item types and categories you define: site, area, asset and scope, or site, unit, system, equipment and CUI circuit. Each asset gets an attributes form generated from its type, a permanent internal tag and an editable real-world tag number. A remediated CUI circuit, for example, shows its inspections, NCRs, handover status and post-handover defects in one timeline.

Registers for staff, vehicles, equipment, consumables and welding procedures use the same model, shown as flat lists with expiry indicators and saved views. Labels can be renamed to match the language your teams already use.

## Key features

- Expandable tree with open-item counts per node and drag-to-reparent or reorder
- Bulk add with naming patterns, start numbers and repeats
- Permanent internal tag plus an editable, conflict-checked tag number
- Asset detail page with attributes, history timeline, inspections, issues, documents, certificates, child assets and location map
- Criticality, custom status and GPS location
- QR, barcode and RFID tags, scan-to-open on mobile, and printable label sheets
- Registers for staff, vehicles, equipment, consumables and WPS with expiry indicators
- "Used in" reverse lookup on register items
- Excel and CSV import with preview, duplicate and tag-conflict detection, mapping templates and rollback
- Merge, split and retag with lineage when equipment is replaced or renumbered
- CUI-specific fields and a comparable corrosion severity grade
- Line, circuit and P&ID linkage, plus risk-based inspection scheduling and condition analytics
