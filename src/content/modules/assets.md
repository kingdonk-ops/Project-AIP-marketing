Assets — not projects — sit at the centre of Project AIP. The asset tree is owned by your organisation, and projects reference assets rather than owning them, so an asset's history survives from one campaign to the next.

Registers for people, equipment, vehicles and consumables use the same model, giving one consistent way to find and report on anything.

## Highlights

- Import large client registers with validation and duplicate detection
- Link assets to drawings and locations
- See the full inspection and defect history of any asset in one place
