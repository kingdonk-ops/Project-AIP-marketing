Contacts & Companies is the shared directory behind the platform: the clients, subcontractors, suppliers and consultants you work with, and the people at each one.

Every person can hold a role on each project, so the same contact can be a client representative on one job and a third-party inspector on another. The directory is used wherever the platform needs to know who someone is — supplier records, transmittal recipients and external portal users.

## Key features

- Companies with their people, roles and contact details
- Roles assigned per project, not fixed per person
- Links from contacts to the projects they work on
- Import from CSV to load existing lists quickly
- Duplicate detection so the same company or person isn't added twice
- One directory shared by procurement, transmittals and the portal
