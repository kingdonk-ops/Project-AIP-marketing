The eligibility gate answers one question for every step that matters: is this person, instrument or material valid right now? It tracks every expiring qualification, calibration certificate, permit, insurance and material use-by date in one place, and blocks work that relies on something that isn't.

Certificates are kept as a real history per item — a thickness gauge builds up a list of calibration certificates over time, and a welder's ticket is replaced rather than overwritten. Status shows green, amber or red, and referencing an expired person or an out-of-calibration instrument is **hard-blocked**. When an exception is genuinely needed, an audited override records who approved it, why, and for how long.

Every sign-off is stamped with a snapshot of the signer's credentials, so years later the record still proves who was qualified at the time. Items marked unavailable or out of stock drop out of reminder feeds automatically, without hiding anything expired that is still in use.

## Key features

- Certificates per person, asset, equipment item, company, material batch or project, with type, number, issuer, expiry, linked document and status
- Expiry-date flag on any attribute field, shown as green, amber or red
- Hard block on expired people and out-of-calibration equipment
- Audited override with a named permission, reason, approver and expiry
- Expiring-soon feed grouped by window, with configurable reminders and supervisor escalation
- Unavailable or out-of-stock items excluded from reminders, and reactivated when they return
- Project register of licences, memberships, professional indemnity, insurance, bonds and permits
- Discipline gating and expired-certificate blocks per inspection category
- Competency matrix per person and per template requirement, with gap report
- Crew and work-package eligibility pre-check
- Calibration schedule and out-for-calibration status for instruments
- Credential snapshots stamped onto every sign-off
