The eligibility gate answers a single question for every step of work: is this person, instrument or material valid right now? It tracks every expiring qualification, calibration certificate, permit, insurance and material use-by date.

Work that relies on something invalid is blocked before it can be signed off, rather than discovered at audit.

## In practice

- A DFT gauge out of calibration can't be used to record a reading
- Inspectors are only suggested for work they are qualified to sign
- Competency gaps are visible before a crew is mobilised
