The portal is a separate, narrow view for people outside your company — clients, subcontractors, suppliers and consultants. They sign in with a magic link and see only what has been explicitly shared with them: assets, reports, inspections awaiting their sign-off, documents and issues in their scope. Commercial modules stay hidden from non-commercial parties, and no admin functions are exposed.

Clients can counter-sign inspections, comment, and confirm or waive attendance at hold and witness points. Subcontractors can respond to NCRs and upload evidence. A named internal owner approves each invitation, and external access expires automatically when the project closes.

## Key features

- Separate, narrow portal with its own session policy and no admin functions
- Client review and counter-sign of inspections
- Hold and witness point booking with attendance confirmation, waive with notice and reminders
- Read-only access to assets, reports and documents in scope, plus a client condition dashboard
- Subcontractor responses to issues and NCRs with controlled evidence upload
- Subcontractor onboarding gate requiring valid company and personnel certificates
- Time-limited share packs usable without a full portal account
- Download watermarking and view-only mode per grant
- Per-client branding and terminology in the portal and notification emails
- "Needs my action" inbox, invitation approval, automatic expiry and full audit of external activity
