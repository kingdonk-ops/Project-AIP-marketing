The site diary is the daily record of what happened on site: weather, labour, plant, events, instructions received, delays, deliveries, photos and video. Labour and plant are pre-filled from your resources and equipment, and weather is fetched automatically, with a manual override when the forecast doesn't match the ground.

At the end of the day the diary is **sealed** into a tamper-evident, signed record. After that nothing is edited — corrections are superseding entries or addenda, and the full history stays visible. Crew members without a full account can still contribute by PIN or magic link, and their entries are flagged for supervisor review.

## Key features

- Daily diary with weather, labour, plant, events, instructions, delays and deliveries
- Photos, video and drone or reality capture attached to the day
- End-of-day seal into a signed, tamper-evident PDF with recorded signer and time
- Superseding corrections and post-seal addenda, with full history
- Field contributions by PIN or magic link, reviewed by a supervisor
- Delay records with cause code, duration, affected scope and notice-sent flag
- Auto-summary of the day's inspections, hold point outcomes and NCRs
- Snapshot of scaffold, isolation and permit status in the day record
- Offline capture, with device time recorded separately from sync time
- Missing-diary alerts and seal-overdue escalation
