Suppliers, Requisitions & POs covers the full purchasing chain in one place: a catalogue of what can be bought from approved suppliers, a requisition cart, approval, purchase order, delivery and invoice.

When a delivery arrives, the PO, delivery docket and invoice sit together on one record with a three-way match. Received items flow into stock, and PO lines can link to cost items, scope tasks and assets, so materials trace to the work they were used on. Price-list updates are reviewed before they publish, so errors don't slip through. Approved POs and matched invoices export to your accounting system.

## Key features

- Supplier catalogue with codes, specs, units, prices, lead times, favourites and recently ordered
- Price lists with validity dates and tiers, imported from supplier spreadsheets
- Import review queue showing per-line changes and percentage flags before publishing
- Requisition cart submitted to accounts, with in-app and email notification, comments and approval
- Purchase orders sent to the supplier by email, with status from draft to closed
- Goods receipt with delivery docket, partial deliveries, batch and serial numbers and material certificates
- Three-way match with tolerance rules and an exception queue
- Supplier eligibility check that blocks PO issue when insurance or accreditation has expired
- Dual approval and verification log for supplier bank-detail changes
- Pricing hidden from subcontractor roles, and export to accounting
