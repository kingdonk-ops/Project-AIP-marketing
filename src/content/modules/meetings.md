Plan a meeting with an agenda and attendees, then record it on a phone, tablet, computer or browser — or upload a recording afterwards. The platform transcribes the audio and drafts the minutes, decisions and actions for you.

Nothing is published on the AI's say-so. The chair reviews the draft with the transcript alongside, confirms or corrects it, and publishes signed minutes. Actions become tasks with an owner and due date, and decisions can be tagged to assets, packages, NCRs and RFIs so meetings become part of the asset history.

## Key features

- Agendas with templates and recurring meeting series, attendees, apologies, minutes, decisions and actions
- Record from any device, upload a recording, or capture offline with resumable uploads
- AI transcription and draft minutes with action extraction, always confirmed by a person before publishing
- Consent workflow: recording notice to all attendees, per-attendee consent, and recording paused if someone declines
- Review editor with source highlights — click a suggested action to hear and see where it came from
- Suggested links to assets, NCRs and RFIs mentioned in the discussion
- Vocabulary hints for NDT, CUI, welding and ITP terms and your project's asset tags
- Toolbox-talk mode with quick PIN or QR sign-on and a single signed attendance record
- Open actions carry forward into the next meeting
- Audio retention rules with automatic deletion after approval and legal-hold override
