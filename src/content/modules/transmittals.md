Transmittals & Correspondence is your own controlled record of which document revisions were sent to whom, when and why, together with the letters, emails, notices and memos exchanged on a project. It complements the client's platform rather than replacing it, and can import external transmittal references.

Issuing a transmittal **locks the exact revisions sent**, records recipients and reason, and tracks acknowledgements and responses, chasing anything overdue. The correspondence register logs inbound and outbound items with parties, threads and cross-references, and contractual notices carry time bars that create tasks and warn before expiry — so both feed directly into change and claims evidence.

## Key features

- Transmittals with automatic numbering, recipients, reason and acknowledgement tracking
- Renamable numbering patterns, reason codes and statuses
- Recipient lists and distribution groups by role, company or package
- Document picker that locks the exact revisions issued; issued records can't be changed
- Asset-linked transmittal lines showing what was sent about each unit or line
- Transmittal cover with a fingerprint per file, plus proof of delivery and an acknowledgement log
- Reissue and supersede chain, highlighting recipients yet to acknowledge
- External recipients acknowledge through a portal link, without a full licence
- Import of external transmittals as read-only records linked to local documents
- Correspondence register for letters, emails, notices and memos, with threads, templates and cross-references
- Contract notice clock with clause time bars, tasks and expiry warnings
- Restricted access for commercial items
