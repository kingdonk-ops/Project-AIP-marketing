The form designer is the no-code builder behind every checklist, inspection, ITP, permit and test report in the platform. Administrators assemble templates from sections and fields, add conditional logic, calculations and validation, and publish a frozen revision.

Inspections in the field always keep the revision they started with, so changing a template never rewrites history.

## Why it matters

- Convert existing paper and spreadsheet checklists into consistent digital forms
- Standard readings are defined once and reused across templates
- Pre-filled context cuts typing and transcription errors on site
