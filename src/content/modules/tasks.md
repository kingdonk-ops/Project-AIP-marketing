Tasks, Deadlines & My Work gives everyone a single home queue: approvals waiting on them, inspections to sign, overdue items, mentions and expiring certificates — each with **inline actions** like Approve, Open and Sign. People can also create simple tasks with a checklist, assignee, due date, priority and an optional asset, so context is never lost.

Behind the queue, a deadline register sweeps due dates from every module, reminds owners and escalates to managers after a grace period. Closing the source record resolves its deadline automatically, and approvals route to a delegate when someone is away.

## Key features

- My Work queue with inline actions (Approve, Open, Sign)
- Simple tasks with checklist, assignee, due date, priority and optional asset; quick-add
- Deadline register across modules: acknowledge, reassign, extend with reason, resolve
- Grace periods and escalation to managers, with audited extensions
- Auto-task rules from hold points reached, NCRs opened, certificates expiring, calibration due and rejected inspections
- Delegation and out-of-office routing for approvals and sign-offs
- Roster- and calendar-aware deadlines that respect public holidays and shutdown periods
- Asset-area task boards and recurring task templates
- Offline task capture and completion, with photos and queued actions
- Overdue and ageing analytics by module, team and escalation level
