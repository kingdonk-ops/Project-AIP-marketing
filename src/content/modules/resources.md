Resources & Crews is a deliberately simple register of the people and crews available to do the work. Each person or crew has skills, availability and assignments, so supervisors can see who is free and who is already committed.

Skills come straight from the competency register, so it's easy to assign people who hold the right tickets. If someone is assigned to two things at once, you get a **double-booking warning** before it becomes a problem on site.

## Key features

- Register of people and crews with skills and availability
- Assign people and crews to scopes of work or inspections
- Double-booking and conflict warnings
- Skills and tickets drawn from the competency register
- Inspector assignment for inspections and hold points
- Assignments shown on the schedule and look-ahead
