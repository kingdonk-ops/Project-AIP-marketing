E-Signatures & Tamper-Evident Records gives inspections, diaries, approvals and published records signatures that stand up to scrutiny. Each sign-off stores an attestation binding the signer's identity, how they authenticated, their qualification, the time and a fingerprint of the exact document they signed.

Published PDFs are **sealed** so they remain verifiable even after certificates expire. Corrections are made as superseding versions, never edits — any change after signing voids the seal and requires a re-sign. External parties can sign through established e-signature providers, and records under retention or legal hold are kept in locked, tamper-evident storage.

## Key features

- Signature captured on the user profile and applied to reports as visible blocks with date, name and role
- Attestation record of who signed, how they authenticated, when and on what document
- Sealed PDFs with long-term validation
- External e-signature provider integration for clients and third parties
- Verification page and manifest for any signed PDF
- Configurable signature meaning and consent statements per record type and market
- Step-up authentication policy for hold points and certificate sign-off
- Signature invalidation on content change, with superseding versions
- Offline sign-off on site with deferred sealing
- Signed evidence bundle export with manifest, file fingerprints and verification instructions
- Signing limited to people with in-date discipline certificates
- Request, sign, decline, void and verify actions
