Stock, Consumables & Materials keeps a live picture of what's on site. Every change is a movement in a ledger — received from a delivery docket, issued to a task, wasted or transferred — so stock levels per location are always current and fully explained.

Issuing consumables to a scope task records who issued them, how much and which batch. That satisfies tasks that **require consumables** before they can proceed, and carries batch and heat numbers into the traceability chain.

## Key features

- Stock items with quantity and unit of measure
- Movements: receipt from delivery docket, issue to task, waste or shrinkage, transfer
- Consumable issuance per scope task with issuer, quantity and batch
- Live stock levels per location and reorder points
- Use-by tracking, with reminders suppressed when an item is out of stock or unavailable
- Batch and heat traceability into the traceability chain
- Tasks flagged as requiring consumables are checked for issuance
