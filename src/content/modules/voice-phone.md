Voice Notes & Phone Log turns what people say into structured records. A worker records or types a note and AI drafts a diary entry, defect or task with the fields filled in. **Nothing is saved until a person confirms it** — the draft can be edited or discarded.

Phone calls and verbal instructions are logged with the parties, direction, duration, summary and the instruction given, linked to the relevant asset or package. Saved entries are locked and corrected only by superseding records, so they hold up as evidence. Recording requires a consent notice, and transcription runs under governed AI data controls.

## Key features

- Voice note to structured draft (diary note, defect or task) with confirm or discard
- Side-by-side transcript and audio with edit history
- Phone call log with parties, direction, duration, summary and follow-up
- Verbal instruction flag that can start a change record and notify the commercial lead
- Confirmation email or task requesting written confirmation of verbal instructions
- Recording consent prompt, with consent stored per record
- Custom vocabulary for NDT, CUI and coating terms, plus asset tags
- Asset and scope suggestions from spoken tag numbers, confirmed by the user
- Option to stop retaining audio after transcription
- Offline voice capture queue with deferred transcription
