Traceability is the core differentiator of Project AIP. It links everything about a piece of work into one chain: the component, the materials and their certificates, the people who installed and inspected it and their qualifications, the instruments used, and the inspection records.

Because each link records whether a welder, instrument or material was valid **at the time it was used**, later expiry can't rewrite history.

## What it unlocks

- Flag a material batch and instantly see every affected component and inspection
- Welder and procedure repair-rate analysis for root-cause work
- Indexed evidence packs per system, asset or spool
