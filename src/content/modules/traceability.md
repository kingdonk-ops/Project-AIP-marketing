Traceability links everything about a piece of work into one searchable chain: the component, the materials and their certificates, the people who installed and inspected it and their qualifications, the equipment used and its calibration, the task, the ITPs and inspections, and the documents.

Because every link is a real reference, you can ask "what has this welder worked on", "where was this batch used" or "which instrument measured this" and get one answer. Genealogy works in both directions — from a heat or batch to every component it went into, and from a component back to everything behind it. Each link records whether the welder, instrument or material was **valid at the time of use**, and that record is frozen, so later expiry or supersession can't rewrite history.

When something is found faulty, one quarantine action on a heat, rod batch or instrument lists every affected component, task and inspection and raises the NCRs. When the client asks for proof, the chain exports as an indexed, bookmarked evidence pack they can verify hasn't changed since issue.

## Key features

- Weld and component records carrying welder, WPS, consumable and steel references
- Material passports with certificate type, number, heat or batch, supplier, grade, linked document, use-by date and status
- Material usage recorded against the asset, work package and task
- "Used in" reverse lookups for people, consumables, equipment and procedures
- Traceability tab on any record, with filters by type and depth
- Search across the chain by heat number, welder or instrument serial, respecting permissions
- Batch quarantine and impact analysis with automatic NCRs
- Point-in-time validity snapshots on every link; corrections add a new link rather than editing
- Weld map and joint register, as a table or on the drawing
- Certificate data extraction with human review, cross-checking heat numbers and grades
- Welder performance and repair-rate analytics by welder, WPS and consumable batch
- Inspection requests for material receipt, work and hidden works, with a request queue
