Change Orders, Variations & MOC keeps every scope change in one register, whatever you call it. A change can start from a notice, an instruction (including a verbal one), an RFI or an inspection finding. It is priced against cost items, approved through the workflow engine and tracked through to final account.

Management of change adds the process-industry review and approval of engineering changes. Labels such as Variation, Change Order, Client Instruction or Scope Change Notice are configurable, so each market and client sees its own terms.

## Key features

- Change register with source, description, asset, cost and time impact, and status
- Variation lifecycle from notice through request, order and measurement to final account
- Daywork sheets built from field records, pulling crew, hours, consumables and plant, with client signature
- Management of change: proposal, technical review, risk review, approval, implementation and close-out
- MOC checklist templates per discipline with required reviewer roles and an open-action gate
- Contractual notice clock with time-bar warnings, with notice periods configurable per contract
- Verbal instruction capture that creates a draft change and a confirmation letter
- Discovered-condition changes raised from inspection findings with photos and asset link
- Links to correspondence, diary, phone log and RFIs
- Approval routes through the workflow engine
