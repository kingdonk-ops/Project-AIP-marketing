Search is built around the asset, not just the document. Look up anything — assets by tag, inspections, issues, reports, documents and correspondence — from one search bar, with filters for asset subtree, project, record type and NDT method. Tag-aware matching handles partial tags, line numbers, spool marks, wildcards and typos, and searches the text inside scanned documents and drawing title blocks so you can find a datasheet by its line number.

For commercial and legal work, structured retrieval filters by party, date window, reference and record type, and builds a dated, referenced evidence pack from the results. Any filter can be saved as a personal, team or project view and reused as a list, a count or a dashboard tile. Every result honours the user's permissions, so nobody sees records they couldn't otherwise open.

## Key features

- Global search across assets, inspections, issues, documents, reports and correspondence
- Keyboard command bar for fast navigation and actions
- Tag-aware asset search with fuzzy and pattern matching
- Full-text search inside scanned documents and drawing title blocks, with hit highlighting
- Facets for record type, asset subtree, project, tag and NDT method, plus recent searches and previews
- Retrieval by party, date window, reference, record type and asset
- Evidence packs with an index, document fingerprints and chain of custody, with a legal hold marker
- Saved views for personal, team or project use, with parameters such as "my area" or "this shutdown"
- Party-level restrictions so privileged material is kept from other parties
- Search follows your own terminology, and every query and export is logged
