Site Logistics & Mobilisation helps you get the site ready to start and control what arrives on it. Each project has a mobilisation plan of readiness items — permits, inductions, site establishment, utilities and access — grouped by area against a target commencement date.

A readiness roll-up shows exactly **what still blocks a start**. Once work is under way, gates with operating hours and slot capacity, laydown zones on a site plan and a delivery booking board linked to purchase orders keep deliveries orderly.

## Key features

- Mobilisation plan with readiness items grouped by area
- Target commencement date per project
- Readiness status roll-up showing what still blocks a start
- Gates with opening hours and delivery slot capacity
- Delivery booking board linked to purchase orders and expected dockets
- Laydown zones on a site plan
- Received goods flow into stock
