A scope of work is a work package executed against an asset. It groups disciplines — welding, coating, NDT and more — each with an ordered chain of tasks. The scope label is renamable, so it can be called a worksheet, work pack or whatever your market uses.

Tasks can require an inspection, ITP, RFI or consumable issue, and these are created automatically from the task. A **hard completion gate** means a scope can't be marked complete until every requirement is in an accepted state, with each gate check stored for audit. Alongside the quality picture, each scope carries its commercial and access data: work order, WBS, priority, planned and earned hours, delays and access method.

## Key features

- Scope records with location, corrosion environment, observation, remedial works, priority and mandated completion date
- Disciplines with ordered, sequenced task chains
- Tasks that auto-create linked ITPs, RFIs and inspections, with one hold point per task
- Progress roll-up and a hard completion gate, with every gate evaluation kept for audit
- Commercial fields: work order, WBS, tier, estimated hours and metres to scan
- Planned versus earned hours, actual and delay hours, and delay reasons such as weather, access, permit and SIMOPS
- Quantity-based progress (square metres, metres, items) alongside hours
- Access method and inspection technique per scope, with procedure references
- Readiness checklist for permits, access, materials, scaffold and competent personnel before work starts
- Templates by asset class and environment, scope revision comparison, and bulk import from client portal exports with a dry run
