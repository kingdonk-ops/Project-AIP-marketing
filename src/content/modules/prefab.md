Prefab & Off-site Manufacture tracks units built away from site — spool pieces, insulation and cladding panels, scaffold components, skids and modules — from design release through fabrication, QA, packing, dispatch, transport, receipt and installation.

Each unit is an asset with an ordered stage register and inspection hold points at the factory and on arrival. Dispatch and installation are blocked while holds, NCRs or expired certificates are open, and when a unit is installed its full fabrication history carries across to the destination asset.

## Key features

- Unit register with type, mark number, revision, destination asset and a status pipeline
- Stage templates per unit type that create the ITP automatically when a unit is registered
- Factory inspections and receipt inspections at defined hold points
- Release-for-dispatch and release-for-install gates that check holds, NCRs and inspector or welder certificates
- Unit bill of materials linked to components, materials and certificates (heat number to unit to asset)
- Receipt inspection on arrival with damage photos and an automatic NCR
- Shipment and load records with packing lists and QR or barcode labels, working offline in the field
- Transport tracking and installation sign-off
- Off-site delivery inspection list and report
- Handover check that all documents, data records and files are complete
