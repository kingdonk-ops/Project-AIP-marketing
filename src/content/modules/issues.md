Issues covers defects, non-conformances and the actions that resolve them. An issue is raised against an asset — often automatically from a failed inspection answer, carrying the step, asset and photos across — then assessed, assigned, fixed, verified and closed.

Non-conformance reports add root-cause analysis and corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), turning quality failures into improvement data.

## Quality enforced by the system

- Open NCRs can hold the related ITP step until dispositioned
- Disposition approvals with engineering and client sign-off
- Structured cause codes for meaningful trend reports
