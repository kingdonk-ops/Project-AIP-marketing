Issues capture the defects and non-conformances found during the work, and the actions that fix them. Each issue is raised against an asset — often directly from a failed inspection answer — with severity, a responsible person, a due date and evidence, then moves through assessment, assignment, rectification, verification and closure.

**NCRs** add the formal layer: classification as material, workmanship or design, root-cause analysis, a disposition of use-as-is, rework, repair or reject with engineering approval, and corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) with their own owners and evidence. While an NCR is open it can place a hold on the related ITP step and asset, which inspections must honour until it is closed.

Configurable rules decide when failed answers raise issues and how severe they are. Subcontractors and clients see only the items scoped to them, and trend reports show where problems keep coming from.

## Key features

- Workflow from open and assessed through action, verification and closure
- Configurable issue types, severities with SLA days, and workflows
- Auto-raise from inspection answers, carrying the source step, asset and photo
- NCR classification, root cause by 5-why or category, and disposition
- Disposition workflow with engineering approval and sealed records
- Corrective and preventive actions with owner, due date, status, evidence and linked task
- NCR hold flag on the ITP step and asset until closure
- Per-issue attachments with photo markup
- Kanban board by status and a filterable register
- Overdue and SLA notifications with escalation, including overdue CAPA
- Root cause categories and trend reports
