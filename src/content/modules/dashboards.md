Dashboards are built for inspection and asset condition rather than generic cost reporting. Each project and portfolio dashboard shows a KPI strip — active scopes, earned versus planned hours, QA/QC backlog, pending client review, ITP completion and expiring certificates — above a tile grid you can configure. Every tile is a saved view, so clicking it opens the filtered register behind the number.

All dashboards, reports, exports and digests read from one permission-aware model, so counts match the registers and only include records the viewer can access. Report templates produce branded, parameterised outputs that are fixed once generated, because issued reports become evidence.

## Key features

- KPI strip with ITP completion, open NCRs, overdue items, expiring certificates and earned versus planned hours
- Configurable tile grid where each tile drills into its filtered register
- Tiles show "restricted" rather than a misleading blank when a user lacks access
- Asset hierarchy heatmap of status by area
- Nightly KPI snapshots with trend charts and period comparisons
- CUI and NDT finding analytics by asset, coating or insulation system, severity and area
- Handover readiness score per asset subtree, combining ITPs, NCRs, certificates and documents
- Report templates with parameters, preview and branded layouts; generated outputs are fixed and fingerprinted
- Scheduled email digests and PDF or Excel distribution, plus threshold alerts per role
- Custom dashboards per user or role
