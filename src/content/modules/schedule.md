Schedule & Look-ahead is a straightforward planning view of what's coming up: inspections that are due, scopes with mandated completion dates, and certificates or calibrations about to expire. It's designed to answer "what do we need to be ready for in the next few weeks?" without a heavyweight planning tool.

A two- to six-week look-ahead shows the work ahead alongside who is assigned to it. Teams can optionally run a **weekly work plan** with commitments, and export the calendar to their own calendar app with reminders.

## Key features

- Calendar of inspections, mandated completion dates and expiries
- Two- to six-week look-ahead
- Optional weekly work plan with commitments
- Assignments shown from resources and crews
- Expiring certificates and calibrations shown on the plan
- Calendar export (iCal) and reminders
