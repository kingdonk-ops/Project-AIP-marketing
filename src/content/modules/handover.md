Handover turns the inspection evidence already on the platform into handover packages, client data books and authority submissions. Rather than collating documents and relying on self-declared status, it assembles **live evidence at asset level** — inspections, NDT and CUI records, and calibration certificates — automatically for each asset or system.

A closeout checklist per project and per asset subtree shows real closure status from punch lists, inspections, NCRs and certificates. A completeness engine compares the ITPs and documents each asset class requires against the signed records that exist, and shows the gaps. Because evidence stays live-linked, reopening an NCR flips the related item back to open.

Each submission is frozen as an immutable snapshot with a hash manifest and signer, and the review cycle with the client or approving authority is tracked through comments, responses and approvals. On acceptance, handover starts the defects liability period and carries a baseline condition snapshot of each asset into service.

## Key features

- Closeout checklist bound to evidence, with percent complete by system or area
- Completeness engine showing missing ITPs and documents by asset subtree
- Data book generation per asset or system, auto-assembled with a hyperlinked index, certificates, weld maps, NDT reports and coating records
- Client-specific data book structures: folder numbering, document codes and naming rules
- Submission templates with field mappings and validation rules
- Export to CSV, Excel, PDF index, JSON and XML
- Asset register handover export mapped to the client's maintenance system import format
- Baseline condition snapshot per asset — wall loss, coating condition and CUI findings — carried into service
- Immutable snapshots with a hash manifest and signer; corrections issued as new revisions
- Resubmission tracking with a comparison of changed documents between pack revisions
- Client and authority review cycle with comments, responses and approvals
- Validation report with click-through to fix the source record, and retention release tied to verified closure
