Handover turns the inspection evidence already in Project AIP into handover packages, client data books and authority submissions — removing weeks of manual compilation at the end of a project.

A completeness view compares the required ITPs and documents for each asset against what is actually signed, so gaps show up early instead of in the close-out crunch.

## Highlights

- Client-specific data book structures defined as configuration
- Immutable issued packs with revision history
- Baseline condition carried into service and maintenance
