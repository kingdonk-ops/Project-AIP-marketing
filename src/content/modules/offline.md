The Offline Field App lets inspectors keep working when there is no signal — on remote mine sites, inside LNG modules, or deep in a plant during a shutdown. Before heading out, each device downloads its assigned assets, active templates, open work and recent history, along with the drawings and documents for the work area.

In the field, inspectors capture inspections, photos, measurements, voice notes and defects exactly as they would online. Every answer is saved as it is entered, and recorded readings are **designed never to be overwritten or lost** — they wait safely in a queue until the device reconnects. Photos upload in the background once coverage returns, with compression and prioritisation to keep storage under control.

When two people change the same field, the app doesn't silently pick a winner: it shows a merge screen so the right value is kept. Qualification and calibration checks also work offline from a downloaded snapshot, then are rechecked on sync, with any sign-off made on since-expired data flagged for review.

## Key features

- Simple field layout — Today, Capture, Inspections and Sync — with a large Capture button for photo, voice, form or defect
- Scan a QR or NFC tag to open an asset, with the last-used asset as the default
- Sync scope covering assigned sites and projects, active templates, open work and recent history
- Per-item sync status and a sync indicator always visible in the header
- Automatic sync on reconnect, on opening the app and at regular intervals, with frequent autosave
- Conflict rules per record type and a field-level merge screen
- Photo markup at capture, with deferred upload when back online
- Offline eligibility snapshot, revalidated on sync
- Offline drawing and document packs per work area, with versions and stale warnings
- Storage budget and media compression controls
- Encrypted on-device storage, device management, and remote wipe for lost devices
- Sync diagnostics and a shareable support bundle
