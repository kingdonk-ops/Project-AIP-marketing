Remote plants, mine sites and shutdowns rarely have reliable coverage. The offline field app lets inspectors keep working with no signal and synchronise when a connection returns.

Records captured offline are clearly marked until they reconcile with the central record, and validity checks — such as whether a person or instrument was qualified — are re-checked at sync time.

## Built for the field

- Pre-download drawings, procedures and ITPs for the area you're working in
- Storage and photo-compression controls for image-heavy work
- Device lock and remote wipe for lost or retired tablets
